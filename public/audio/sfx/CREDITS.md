## Still synthesised (no recording fits)

`vox.*` (grunts, cries, effort), `bow.twang`, `sling.whirl`, `sling.release`, the three instrument stingers (`stinger.*`: cornu, kithara, syrinx, which suit Rome better than any UI chime), and the ambience events (`amb.*`) and beds for fire, wind, city, cicadas and crickets. These are baked in code (`src/audio/sounds/`), as are the stand-ins used while a recording loads or if it cannot.
