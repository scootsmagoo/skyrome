# Realistic bodies (male.glb, female.glb) and their maps (public/textures/people)

Derived from **Human Base Meshes** (bundle v1.4.1), Blender Studio.

- Source: https://studio.blender.org/projects/human-base-meshes/ (download: https://mirror.blender.org/demo/asset-bundles/human-base-meshes/human-base-meshes-bundle-v1.4.1.zip)
- Author: Blender Studio (Blender Foundation), Human Base Meshes team.
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), as published with the bundle.
- Objects used: `GEO-body_male_realistic`, `GEO-body_female_realistic` (the sculpted multires levels are baked into
  the normal and AO maps; the geometry is decimated to 3 LODs and re-posed to the game's bind pose).

The whole pipeline is in `tools/characters/` (`npm run characters`). Everything in this folder is generated by it.
