# The big rework (October 2026): graphics, audio, map, physics

The owner's brief:
- Characters are "garbage".
- The graphics are "mid".
- The audio is "awful": the music is "blaring synth", the SFX are weak, and footsteps are bad on anything but streets.
- The map has dead-ends, curbs that pedestrians get stuck on, floating objects and wrong place names.
- NPCs are too easy to knock down when running.
- A load carried on the head should fall separately and be picked up.

The goal is **as realistic as possible while holding performance** (60 fps on an M-series MacBook in Safari and Chrome; fewer than about 1500 draws and about 3 M visible triangles; the Forum is at 4 M today).

**Shared rules for every workstream:**
- **Measure before and after:** `node scripts/perf.mjs` and `scripts/shot.mjs`, and look at the PNGs.
- **Keep the existing tests green.** Add tests for pure logic.
- **CC0 assets only.** Record provenance in `public/<kind>/CREDITS.md` (source URL, author, licence quoted).

## Workstreams, wave 1

Each workstream runs in its own git worktree on its own branch. Shared files are listed per workstream. Where two workstreams meet, the contract is written here.

### C0: realistic characters (pipeline and prototype)
- **Source:** Blender Studio Human Base Meshes v1.4.1 (CC0), from `https://mirror.blender.org/demo/asset-bundles/human-base-meshes/human-base-meshes-bundle-v1.4.1.zip`, kept outside the repo in `.cache/` (gitignored).
  - Used: `GEO-body_male_realistic` and `GEO-body_female_realistic` (about 21k triangles each, UVs, Multires sculpt levels) and their eye meshes.
- **Tool:** `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --python tools/characters/<script>.py` (Blender 5.1).
- **Pipeline** (`tools/characters/`, committed and reproducible; `npm run characters` rebuilds):
  1. **Dump the game's rig.** Write the reference rigs from `computeRig` (male average 1.75 m; female average) as JSON: `tools/characters/dump-rig.test.ts` or a node script using `tsx`.
  2. **Find the base mesh's joints.** Measure the HBM body's joint positions (cross-section analysis, or hand-checked landmark coordinates in `tools/characters/landmarks.json`).
  3. **Rig and re-pose.** Build a temporary armature on the HBM joints, auto-weight it (heat), then pose it into the game's bind pose: **arms hanging straight down** (shoulder, elbow and wrist share x), legs straight, at the game's joint positions for the reference rig. Apply the pose (bake it into the mesh).
  4. **Bake maps from the Multires levels:** a tangent-space normal map, plus AO/cavity at 2048 (LOD0) and 1024 (LOD1).
  5. **Decimate into LODs:** LOD0 about 10–12k triangles, LOD1 about 4k, LOD2 about 1.2k. Keep the UVs.
  6. **Export** GLB with POSITION, NORMAL, TANGENT, UV, and JOINTS_0/WEIGHTS_0 whose joint names are the game's `BONES` (the 25 names in `src/actors/avatar/rig.ts`). Textures go out as KTX2 (`basisu`, installed). Output goes to `public/models/people/{male,female}.glb` and `public/textures/people/*.ktx2`.
- **Runtime** (`src/actors/avatar/real/**`, new):
  - Load the GLB.
  - Build a `THREE.SkinnedMesh` that uses the **game's own skeleton**: identity rest rotations, translation-only inverses from `computeRig`. The GLB's skin is used only for the vertex weights, remapped by bone name.
  - **Proportion morph** per appearance:
    - each vertex moves by the weighted deltas between the target rig's joints and the reference rig's joints;
    - build and girth are a per-bone radial scale around the bone axis;
    - heights come from rig.s.
  - **Material:** `MeshStandardMaterial` with the baked normal and AO maps, patched in `onBeforeCompile` with:
    - wrap-lighting subsurface for skin (terminator tint; ears and nose back-light);
    - skin tint from the appearance;
    - a procedural pore and micro-variation detail normal;
    - specular AA.
  - Eyes are separate meshes with a cornea highlight.
- **Prototype scope (wave 1):** `?scene=avatars&avatar=real` shows the real body for male and female at several heights and builds, animated by the existing controller (walk, run, idle, attack), next to the classic avatar. The classic avatar stays the default until wave 2.
- **Acceptance:**
  - screenshots at 2 m, 6 m and 20 m look like real people (anatomy, shading), with no skinning tears at shoulders, elbows, hips and knees through the walk, run and attack clips;
  - triangle counts per LOD as above;
  - a perf note: 30 real bodies on screen, CPU and GPU ms.
- **Not in wave 1:** garments, hair, faces painted, helmets, gore. Those are wave 2, with the contract recorded in `docs/modules/avatar-real.md`, which C0 writes.

### C1: animation quality (`src/actors/avatar/anim/**`)
- **Foot contacts.** Emit foot-contact events from the gait phase (0 = left contact, 0.5 = right): `AvatarView.onFootContact?: (side: 'L' | 'R', gait: string, strength: number) => void`. This is a new optional hook, set by consumers. A3 (audio) uses it.
- **Secondary motion:** critically damped springs on the forearms, hands, head, and carried kit (weapon, shield, torch, props), driven by root acceleration and turn rate. There is a per-gait amount and none in first person.
- **Fix the sprint direction lock** (`controller.ts:651-654`).
- **Runtime foot IK within 40 m:** a ray per foot (`Layer.World`), with a pelvis drop and ankle correction on stairs, curbs and slopes. It must not fight the reach-limited hip curve. Cap the cost.
- **Directional hit reactions** (left and right), as an additive impulse scaled by the impact vector.
- **Acceptance:**
  - the avatar tests stay green, plus new tests for the contact events and IK;
  - screenshots on a stair and a kerb show planted feet;
  - no cost regression over 0.05 ms per avatar.

### R1: rendering, light (renderer, `src/gfx/post/**`, `src/world/sky/**`, `src/core/graphics.ts`)
- **Tone mapping:** AgX or Neutral (A/B both, pick by screenshots), retuned exposure, and a colour grade (a warm Mediterranean grade with gentle shadow desaturation) as a 3D LUT built in code.
- **IBL** from the sky: PMREM refreshed on a timer or sun change, crossfaded. A diffuse SH or irradiance term in place of the ad-hoc hemisphere light.
- **Shadows:** two cascades on High (near 0–30 m at 2048, far to 150 m at 2048), or a tight player and character map. Soft edges (PCF soft). Only near characters cast.
- **Contact shadows:** screen-space contact shadows (8–12 steps) on High and blob shadows under characters on all tiers.
- **AO:** on Medium as well, at half resolution and with a radius tuned for people and kerbs.
- **Fog:** aerial perspective, at least a view-dependent fog colour tied to the sky model.
- **Acceptance:** before/after screenshots at the Forum 10:00, the Circus 18:00 and night. GPU ms within +1.5 ms on High at the same views. Medium stays ≤ 6.5 ms at the Forum.

### R2: rendering, materials and vegetation (`src/gfx/materials*`, `src/gfx/textures/**` shader patches, trees and vegetation, terrain shading)
- **Specular AA** (roughness from normal variance) in the shared patch.
- **Anisotropy** at the maximum (16).
- **Detail normals** at 4–8× scale on stone, plaster, paving and cloth.
- **Macro variation and wear on paving and walls:**
  - wear in the street centre;
  - dirt at wall bases (the existing grime band works with this);
  - colour variation per stone.
- **Trees:** foliage translucency and wind sway (per-instance phase). Improve the tree shapes: they read as "blobs of green". Use leaf-card canopies with alpha-to-coverage where MSAA is on.
- **Water:** improve the Tiber and fountains if cheap.
- **Acceptance:** before/after close-ups (wall at 2 m, street at 5 m, trees at 20 m), and GPU within +1 ms.

### A1: sound effects, footsteps, mix (`src/audio/**` except `music/**`, `public/audio/sfx/**`)
- **Assets:**
  - **Sources:** Kenney Impact Sounds, RPG Audio and Interface Sounds (CC0), and the OpenGameArt CC0 packs:
    - "Different steps on wood, stone, leaves, gravel and mud";
    - Fantasy Weapons and Apparel;
    - the StarNinjas sword and clash sets;
    - Swishes;
    - 100 CC0 SFX #2;
    - Crowd Shouting/Speaking ambience;
    - Ambient Bird Sounds;
    - water splash and sand footsteps.
  - Verify each licence on the page.
  - **Convert** with ffmpeg to mono AAC `.m4a` at 96–128 kbps, with an `.mp3` fallback.
  - **Normalise loudness** (peak or LUFS), trim silences, and build a manifest with provenance.
- **Loader:** fetch, then `decodeAudioData`, behind the existing bank contracts, with prefetch of footsteps and common combat sounds.
- **Footsteps:** surfaces stone, marble (bright stone), cobbles, gravel, dirt, grass, wood, sand and water.
  - Variants: 8–12 round-robin per surface, with pitch and gain variation, and heel/scuff layers.
  - Gear layers: hobnailed caligae on stone, armour and cloth.
  - **NPCs and combatants use the real surface lookup,** sampled at a low rate (today they are always stone).
  - Steps are triggered from `AvatarView.onFootContact` when present (the C1 contract), with the speed cadence as the fallback.
- **Combat:** clash, flesh, punch, block (shield and metal), body fall, bow twang, arrow whoosh and impacts.
  - Map every sfx id the game requests (the scout's list) to samples.
  - Keep the synthesis only where no sample fits.
- **UI and world:** UI clicks, quest and level stingers, doors, chests, pickups and coins.
- **Mix:**
  - re-level everything;
  - master glue and limiter retuned;
  - ducking for dialogue;
  - expose the ambience, voice and UI volume sliders in Settings.
- **Ambience:** replace the synthetic crowd babble and birds with the CC0 recordings where available (streamed beds).
- **Acceptance:** a listening checklist in the report (what each sound is, its source). `game.audio.meter()` peaks under −1 dBFS in a fight. Memory reported (`game.audio.stats()`). The audio tests rewritten for samples.

### A2: music (`src/audio/music/**`, `public/audio/music/**`)
- **Replace the synthesised timbres** (the Aulos sawtooth, the Drone) with sampled instruments from **VSCO 2 Community Edition** (CC0; verify the licence and pick a small subset):
  - harp for the lyre/kithara;
  - oboe for the aulos (a double reed), played softly;
  - alto flute or recorder for the syrinx;
  - frame drum, tambourine or hand percussion for the tympanum;
  - a soft low string or choir pad if available.
- **Keep the generative composer** (modes, seeds, states), but make it gentle: slower attacks, sparse phrases, a low-pass on the bus, the music trim at 0 dB or lower, combat velocity capped, and long silences.
- **Render the Epitaph of Seikilos** and the First Delphic Hymn (public-domain MIDI on Wikimedia Commons) with the sampled lyre as set pieces (temples, the Lemuria night, the title).
- **Acceptance:** a description of each music state; levels measured with `meter()`; memory budget ≤ 16 MB decoded; and the owner's words "assault on the ears" no longer apply (no sawtooth, no harsh 1–3 kHz peaks).

### M1: place names (`src/data/atlas.ts` names, `src/content/places.ts`, `src/game/locations.ts`, `src/ui/map*`)
- **One name pipeline** for banners, map, compass and journal: `displayName` everywhere, with no raw parentheticals reaching the player.
- **Unique display names:** fix the collisions (Temple of Castor and Pollux in the Forum and at the Circus Flaminius; the two Isis sanctuaries; the two Arches of Augustus; the two Ulpian libraries).
- **No modern or medieval names in player text.** These go to `codexNote`: "Vatican Pyramid", "Mound of Potsherds", "Seven Halls Cistern", "formerly Nero", "Parthian Arch", "Passageway Forum".
- **Verify suspect Latin** against sources (for example `temple-apollo-sosianus` "(Medici)"). The map and the banners must agree.

### M2: traversal and grounding (`src/world/city/**` curbs and props, `src/arch/fabric/streets.ts`, `src/ai/life/navgrid.ts`, `src/actors/Actor.ts` constants)
- **One traversal contract module** holding `STEP_MAX`, the KCC autostep, the assist, and `NavGrid.maxStep`. Align them: the planner never asks an NPC to climb what the actor can't.
- **One kerb standard:** a 0.15 m curb, one sidewalk datum per block, and ramps at every street end and at crossings. Unify the lifts so no 2–6 cm lips appear between surfaces.
- **NPC stuck logging:** record every `unstick` with position, district and edge. Add a soak report.
- **Prop grounding:** every prop placement samples its footprint corners and sits on the surface, with a capped slope. Fix the callers in `roads.ts`, `fill.ts` and `life.ts`.
- **Audits as tests:** curb height ≤ the step contract, prop base within 0.06 m of the surface under its corners, no traversal edge the actor can't climb.
- **Acceptance:** a crawl before and after (`scripts/crawl.mjs`) with lip, floating and buried counts down sharply; a soak with stuck counts reported.

### M3: dead-ends (`src/world/city/plan.ts`, `network.ts`, `roads.ts` road gaps)
- **Close the minor-street stubs.** Extend each stub toward the nearest ROAD or STREET within about 15 m, or end it in a piazza or court with a reason.
- **Bridge road gaps** inside solid footprints (houses, tombs) with plaza squares. Add road crossings at the Servian gates.
- **Stairs to nowhere:** connect them, or mark them as deliberate termini.
- **Street-end audit test** (every street end meets ROAD, STREET, PIAZZA or PLAZA with a link) and a graph test (no degree-1 nodes that aren't spots).
- **Acceptance:** stub count before and after, and screenshots of 5 former dead-ends.

### P1: knockdowns, falls and carried loads (`src/npc/**` shove, brain and props, `src/physics/ragdoll/**`, `src/combat/CombatCore.ts` knockdown wiring, `src/actors/avatar/anim/actions.ts` for a ground pickup clip only)
- **Physical topple gate:**
  - resolved once per contact, with a 2–3 s per-NPC cooldown;
  - the impulse comes from the player's momentum (about 75 kg × approach speed) against the NPC's resistance (build, age, carried load, braced or not, facing);
  - below the threshold the NPC stumbles (a short partial-strength ragdoll or the stagger clip), above it they fall.
  - Running into people rarely fells them; a full sprint into a slight person can.
- **Melee knockdown wired** (power plus heavy, not braced). NPC poise by tier and archetype.
- **Downed state in the brain:** pauses the mover and stuck ladder, then a short dazed beat and a bark after getting up.
- **Ragdoll mass** calibrated to about 70–75 kg. Ragdolls collide with characters if it's stable, otherwise documented.
- **Carried loads:**
  - On a knockdown or death, a head, back or chest load (basket, amphora, sack, tray) detaches into its own rigid body (`Layer.Debris`), seeded with the bone's velocity, and can break (amphora shards) or spill.
  - **The NPC walks to it, crouches, picks it up** (a new ground-reach pickup clip) and puts it back on. There is a timeout, and the NPC gives up if it's gone.
  - The budget follows the `GoreSystem` pattern.
- **Acceptance:** tests for the gate (thresholds, cooldown) and the recover task. In-game shots of a basket on the ground and the NPC picking it up. Physics cost reported.
