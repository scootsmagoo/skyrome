Cause: the host's inotify watch limit. The game ran at full speed regardless. Fix on the host (raise `fs.inotify.max_user_watches`, which needs sudo, so Murphy must run it) or exclude `docs/research/` from the watcher. Log: lines 1 to 2124 of `playtest-2026-11-02.log`, first at line 1.

The `--stuck` file `/tmp/stuck-2026-11-02.json` reports `total: 0`, and the end-of-run summary says `NPC unsticks: 0 total`. Those two disagree with the column, and the log does not say why. Needs a look at how `soak.mjs` counts "stuck" versus logs an unstick. Log: minute 14 and 15 lines (`trips 73 (stuck 24)`, `trips 85 (stuck 36)`).
3. **Frame rate.** One sample dipped to 41 fps (minute 10). None fell below 30 fps.
4. **Deaths.** Three deaths, logged as `[soak] player died` after minutes 4, 9 and 14 (at 6,9,-24; -19,8,28; -36,12,3). Each was rescued (`rescued (1/2/3 so far)`), and the death counter matched recoveries in every sample.
