The bot kept returning to 26,8,0 (rescues at minutes 5, 9 and 14 are near there or at it), so it looks stuck there for about five minutes. The bot is not confirmed frozen, and the cause is not known. Log lines 9 to 19.
2. **Bot stuck counter vs NPC unstick log disagree.** The per-minute "stuck" counter rose to 71 by minute 12 (bot's own trip-progress counter, `soak.mjs`), but the NPC unstick report printed "0 total" (log line 22) and `/tmp/stuck-2026-10-18.json` contains `total: 0`. The two counters measure different things, so this may not be a bug, but it is worth confirming which one is right. The same mismatch appeared in the 2026-10-14 run.
3. **Deaths: 3, all recovered.** Rescues logged at minutes 4-5 (-7,9,39), minute 9 (26,9,30) and minute 14 (26,8,0). No unrecovered deaths.

Errors: `grep -i error|warn|uncaught|stuck` on the log returned only the per-minute counters and the summary line. No error, warning or uncaught exception lines. Page errors: 0. Crashed: false.
