## Findings

1. **No game errors.** The grep for `error`, `warn`, `Uncaught` and `stuck` finds no page error, no uncaught exception and no NPC stuck event. The soak's own `errors 0` agrees.
2. **Bot trip stalls rose across the run.** The stalled trip count went from 0 at minute 1 to 34 at minute 15 (log lines 2125 to 2142). Most of the rise came between minutes 4 and 9, which follows the first death at minute 5. The log does not say whether these come from the planner or the bot's own steering. Not investigated.
3. **Environment noise, not a game bug.** The log has 2123 `file watcher error: ENOSPC` lines from the Vite dev server. The host's inotify watch limit is reached while Vite watches `docs/research/solid-ground/before/`. These lines did not affect the run. Raising `fs.inotify.max_user_watches` on the host would silence them.
