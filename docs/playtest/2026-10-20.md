## Findings

No errors, warnings or uncaught exceptions in the log (grep for `error`, `warn`, `Uncaught`: no matches; verdict line reports `errors 0`).

1. **Low-FPS sample at minute 10 (32 fps).** Not below the 30 fps threshold. Minutes 4, 8 and 11 were 44 to 48 fps. Headless run, so this may be the sandbox GPU rather than the game. Worth watching in later runs.
2. **Stuck counter rises with trips (18 by minute 15).** The per-minute `stuck` figure in the samples grows steadily. The soak's own summary says `NPC unsticks: 0 total (0 logged)`, and `/tmp/stuck-2026-10-20.json` has `total: 0`. The two counters disagree, so the `stuck` figure in the samples may count something other than NPC unsticks. Not resolved here. Needs a look at what the soak counts as stuck.
3. **Death recovery works.** Three deaths (minutes 4, 9, 14), each logged as "rescued" with the count incremented. Player HP was back to 100 after each. Minute 15 ends at hp 86.
