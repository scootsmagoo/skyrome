## Findings

1. **Stuck counter rose with no NPC unsticks logged.** The bot's stuck count went from 1 (min 1) to 24 (min 14), with most of the rise from minute 12 on (9 → 24). The soak's NPC unstick line reads `NPC unsticks: 0 total (0 logged, 0.0 per minute)`, and `/tmp/stuck-2026-10-23.json` holds `{"total": 0, "log": []}`. Either the sample's "stuck" column counts something other than NPC stuck events, or NPC stuck events are not being logged. Log lines: sample lines 2–17 and the `NPC unsticks` line (near the end of the log). Not resolved; needs a look at what the column measures.
2. **FPS dipped to 36 at minute 15** (sample 15), after 54–60 in minutes 1–14. This is above the 30 fps threshold, so it is not a dip below 30. It was not repeated at the sampled minutes; sub-minute dips are not visible in the log.
3. **Host file-watcher limit hit at boot.** The log opens with about 2,118 `file watcher error: ENOSPC: System limit for number of file watchers reached` lines from the dev server. They are host inotify exhaustion, not game errors, and the soak counted 0 errors. They may still mean the dev server ran without file watching. Fix is on the host, not in the repo.

Deaths: 3 (minutes 5, 9–10, 14), all rescued by the Aesculapian rescue, as logged (`player died ...; rescued (N so far)`). No recovery failures.

No `Uncaught`, `warn` or page error lines outside the watcher noise.
