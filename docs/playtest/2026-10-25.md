## Findings

1. **FPS drops to 30 at minutes 14 and 15.** Both samples read 30, which is the lower bound of the 30 fps threshold, not below it. The run was at 50–60 fps before that. Cause not determined from this log.
2. **Stuck counter rises late in the run.** The per-minute stuck count went from 10 at minute 13 to 20 at minute 14 and 34 at minute 15. The same window has the fps drop. The `--stuck` JSON at `/tmp/stuck-2026-10-25.json` says `total: 0` and an empty log, and the log's own footer says `NPC unsticks: 0 total`. The sample counter and the stuck report disagree, so the stuck count may be a bot-side counter, not confirmed NPC stuck events. Needs a look at how `soak.mjs` counts stuck.
3. **File watcher errors (environment, not game).** The log has 2124 lines like `file watcher error: ENOSPC: System limit for number of file watchers reached` for the skyrome tree (including `docs/research/solid-ground/` and `tests/`). The inotify limit is `max_user_watches = 524288`, so the dev server's watcher ran out, probably because many agent worktrees and dev servers share the host. It did not affect the PASS verdict. It may affect HMR during dev work on this host.

## Errors and grep results

- `grep -i error|warn|uncaught|stuck`: the only hits are the ENOSPC watcher lines (2124) and the stuck samples above. No `Uncaught`, no page errors, no crash.
