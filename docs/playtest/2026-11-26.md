Single-minute sample, coinciding with a save/load cycle (saves 1 to 2, loads 0 to 1). Not reproduced in the run. The minute-6 (30 fps) and minute-2 (29 fps) samples are at the threshold but not below it. Whether the 18 fps is real or a bot-side artefact is not determined.
2. **Deaths.** Three player deaths (minutes 4-5, 8-9, 13-14), each logged as rescued (`[soak] player died at 0,8,52; rescued (1 so far)`, and the 2nd and 3rd). All three recovered. Recovery is not timed in the log.

The end-of-run summary says `NPC unsticks: 0 total (0 logged, 0.0 per minute)`, and `/tmp/stuck-2026-11-26.json` has `"total": 0, "log": []`. The minute-line counter appears to track something other than NPC unsticks. Its meaning is not confirmed here, so it is reported as recorded and not interpreted.
4. **No errors.** The grep for `error|warn|uncaught|stuck|page` matched only the sample lines and the summary. No page errors, uncaught exceptions or warnings were logged. `errors 0` in the verdict.
5. **Minor.** HP dipped to 93 at minutes 12 and 15. Nothing else in the log.
