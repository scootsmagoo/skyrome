   - Log lines: 4, 7, 9, 13 of `playtest-2026-11-21.log`.
2. **Stuck counter does not match the NPC unsticks report.** The trip line's `(stuck N)` climbs from 1 to 13. The stuck JSON written to `/tmp/stuck-2026-11-21.json` has `total: 0`, and the end-of-run summary says `NPC unsticks: 0 total (0 logged)`. The two counters measure different things, or one is wrong. Unexplained; needs a look at what `(stuck N)` counts in `scripts/soak.mjs`.
   - Log lines: 2 (minute 1 trip line) through 17 (minute 14), and 20 (`NPC unsticks: 0 total`).
3. **Deaths: 3, all recovered.** The Aesculapian rescue ran at minutes 5, 10 and 15 (log lines 6, 12 and 18 of `playtest-2026-11-21.log`); the log counts each as recovered.
   - Log lines: 6, 12, 18.
