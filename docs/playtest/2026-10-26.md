   - Minute 4–5: `[soak] player died at 5,9,-23; rescued (1 so far)`
   - Minute 9: `[soak] player died at 16,8,1; rescued (2 so far)`
   - Minute 14: `[soak] player died at 16,8,1; rescued (3 so far)`
   Two of the three deaths happened at the same spot (16,8,1), and the bot's reported position sat at 16,8,1 for roughly minutes 7–14 with trips and fights flat. This may be the bot idling or re-dying in one place rather than a game fault. The log does not say which, so this needs a look.

2. **Stuck counter disagrees with the unstick summary.** The per-minute line reports `stuck 3` from minute 5 onward. The end-of-run summary says `NPC unsticks: 0 total (0 logged)`, and `/tmp/stuck-2026-10-26.json` reports `"total": 0, "log": []`. The bot's stuck counter and the NPC unstick logger do not agree. Which one is right is not determined from this log.
