## Findings

1. **Frame rate dipped below 30 fps in two samples.** Minute 5 read 28 fps and minute 7 read 25 fps (log lines `minute  5:` and `minute  7:`). Minute 10 read 29 fps. These are single-sample readings from a headless browser on a software or shared GPU, so they may not reflect real hardware. Not confirmed as a game regression.
2. **Stuck counts rose in the second half.** The per-minute stuck count went from 10 at minute 10 to 31 at minute 15 (log lines `minute 13:` to `minute 15:`). The soak's own NPC unstick summary reads `NPC unsticks: 0 total`, and the `--stuck` JSON written to `/tmp/stuck-2026-11-01.json` has `total: 0`. The two counters disagree, so either the per-minute "stuck" is a different counter (bot trip stuck) or the NPC unstick logging missed events. Needs a look at how `soak.mjs` counts stuck.
3. **Deaths were all recovered.** The bot died three times, at roughly minutes 5, 9 and 14 (`[soak] player died …; rescued (1/2/3 so far)`). Each was rescued. HP returned to 100 after each.
4. **Environment noise, not a game error.** The log has 2124 lines of `file watcher error: ENOSPC: System limit for number of file watchers reached` from the Vite dev server. This is the host's inotify limit, not game code. Raising `fs.inotify.max_user_watches` on the runner would clear it.
