## Findings

1. **Bot-side stuck trips (info).** The bot's own "stuck" counter rose from 1 to 20 across the run (a goal not reached while still for more than 40 frames). Log lines: minutes 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 12 and 13 in `playtest-2026-10-15.log`. This counter is the bot's, not the NPCs'. Its sum does not match the NPC unstick report, which logged 0 unsticks, so the cause is probably the bot's own pathing or its goal choice, not NPC stuck events. Not confirmed.
2. **One frame-rate dip (info).** Minute 10 sampled at 37 fps, back to 60 fps the next minute. The minimum-fps value is not printed, so there is no evidence of a dip below 30 fps.
3. **Deaths (info).** Three deaths, at minutes 5, 9 and 14, each rescued by the Aesculapian rescue (3 of 3 recovered). Heap was flat through the deaths.

No page errors, no `Uncaught` lines and no `error` or `warn` lines in the log.
