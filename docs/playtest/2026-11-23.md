## Findings

1. **No crashes, no page errors, no uncaught exceptions.** Log grep for `error`, `warn`, `Uncaught` and `fail` outside the sample lines returned nothing.
2. **Heap:** 548 MB at minute 1, 575 MB at minute 13. Growth is 27 MB, under the 100 MB limit. Heap moves up and down between samples, so this is not a clear leak trend in 15 minutes.
3. **FPS dips below 30:** minute 1 (26 fps), minute 3 (26 fps), minute 13 (29 fps). These are headless software-rendered numbers and may not match a real GPU.
4. **Deaths:** 3 deaths (minutes ~4–5, ~9–10, ~14). Each was rescued by the Aesculapian rescue path (log: `[soak] player died ...; rescued (N so far)`). All 3 recovered.
5. **Stuck events:** the sample counter rose from 1 to 10. The stuck JSON (`/tmp/stuck-2026-11-23.json`) reports `total: 0` and an empty log, and the summary says "NPC unsticks: 0 total". The two sources disagree, so the stuck counter needs a look before anyone relies on it. The log shows no further detail.
6. **Log lines:** the full log is at `/home/murphy/.hermes/cache/scratch/playtest-2026-11-23.log` on the playtest host, not in the repo.
