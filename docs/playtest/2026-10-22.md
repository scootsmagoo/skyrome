## Findings

1. **FPS dips.** Minute 10 fell to 25 fps, below the 30 fps line. Minutes 13 to 15 ran at 39, 44 and 41 fps. The log has no cause for either. Minute 3 (48 fps) is the only other dip. A single 25 fps minute may be a shadow or streaming spike, or the sandbox's other processes. This needs a profile before anyone calls it a regression.
   - Log line 2137: `minute 10: heap 555 MB · 25 fps · trips 61 (stuck 26) · ...`
   - Log line 2140: `minute 13: heap 574 MB · 39 fps · ...`

2. **Stuck count disagrees between two outputs.** The per-minute counter climbed to 26 stuck events by minute 10. The stuck file `/tmp/stuck-2026-10-22.json` says `"total": 0` with an empty log. The soak summary says `NPC unsticks: 0 total (0 logged, 0.0 per minute)`. One of the two counters is wrong, or they count different things. Not resolved here. The 26 events in the minute samples are the only sign of stuck NPCs in this run.
   - Log line 2137: `(stuck 26)`
   - Log line 2150: `NPC unsticks: 0 total (0 logged, 0.0 per minute)`

3. **Deaths and rescues.** Three deaths (minutes 5, 9 to 10, and 14 to 15), all rescued by the Aesculapian rescue. The log shows `[soak] player died at -9,10,-41; rescued (1 so far)`, `… at -17,8,35; rescued (2 so far)` and `… at 45,8,-10; rescued (3 so far)`. Recovery worked each time, and HP was back to 100 by the next sample.

4. **Log noise, not a game error.** The log has many `file watcher error: ENOSPC: System limit for number of file watchers reached` lines from the Vite dev server watching `docs/research/solid-ground/`. This is the host's inotify limit, not game code. The log has no `Uncaught`, no page errors and no `error` entries from the game. The grep for `error`/`warn` matched only the watcher lines.
