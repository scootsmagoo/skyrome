The soak still passed, but the host inotify limit should be raised (`fs.inotify.max_user_watches`) or the dev server should ignore `docs/`. The first one is on line 1 of the playtest log.
2. **Bot stuck counter rose to 18 trips, but NPC unsticks were 0.** The log reports `NPC unsticks: 0 total (0 logged, 0.0 per minute)` and the stuck JSON at `/tmp/stuck-2026-11-08.json` has `total: 0`. The bot's own "stuck" trip counter (the number in brackets in the samples) went from 3 to 18. The two counters disagree and the cause is not known from this run. Needs a look at what the bot counts as stuck.
3. **Fps dip to 53 at minute 11.** Above the 30 fps threshold and short. Not a blocker.

No page errors, no `Uncaught` lines, no crashes. Grep for `error`, `warn`, `Uncaught` and `stuck` shows only finding 1 and the summary lines.
