## Findings

1. **Frame rate dipped below 30 fps at sampled minutes 1 (28), 3 (24), 5 (28), 7 (28) and 8 (22).** The soak prints one fps value per minute and does not say whether it is a mean or a minimum, so these are sample readings, not a verified sustained drop. Headless software-GPU runs are noisy, so treat this as a lead to check on real hardware, not a confirmed regression.
2. **Player trips with no progress ("stuck") rose from 2 to 9 over the run.** These are the bot's own walking trips where the player made no progress for over 40 frames. This is not the NPC unstick counter, which reported 0. The two numbers measure different things and the cause of the trip stalls is not identified from this log.
3. **Deaths: 3, all rescued.** The Aesculapian rescue fired each time (log lines: deaths at 12,11,-81; 15,9,30; -29,8,33). Recovery worked in every case.
4. **Saves and loads:** 3 saves and 1 load completed. No errors were reported.
