## Findings

1. **Stuck counts are high but the unstick count is zero.** The trip column reached 22 stuck events, while the summary reads `NPC unsticks: 0 total (0 logged, 0.0 per minute)`. The stuck file `/tmp/stuck-2026-10-16.json` reports `"total": 0, "log": []`. The two counters disagree. The bot's stuck counter may count something other than the NPC unstick logger, or the unstick logger may not be wired to this path. Log line 20 and the `/tmp` JSON are the evidence. Needs a look by whoever owns the soak script.
2. **Frame rate dips to 48 fps** at minutes 5 and 8 (log lines 7 and 10). These stay above the 30 fps line, so nothing failed. Recorded for the record only.
3. **Deaths were all rescued.** Three deaths at minutes 5, 9 and 14 (log lines 6, 12 and 18), each logged as rescued. The bot's death recovery worked each time.
4. **No errors.** No `error`, `warn`, `Uncaught` or page-error lines in the log. Only the summary line reports `errors 0`.
