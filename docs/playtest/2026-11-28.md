2. **Stuck counter rises to 37 over the run, but the NPC unstick counter reads 0.** The per-minute `stuck` value climbs from 0 to 37, and most of the rise is between minutes 3 and 7. The soak summary line says `NPC unsticks: 0 total (0 logged, 0.0 per minute)`, and the `--stuck` JSON file is empty (`"total": 0, "log": []`). The two counters disagree, so the stuck count may be the bot's own trip-stuck detector and not NPC events. Needs a look at what the soak counts as "stuck".
