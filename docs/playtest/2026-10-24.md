## Findings

1. **Frame rate fell to 26-30 fps in minutes 13-15** (log lines for minutes 13, 14 and 15). Minutes 1-12 held 50-60 fps. The fall came with the bot's trip and stuck counts climbing fastest (trips 72 → 107, stuck 47 → 78 in those minutes) and with `colliders` steady at about 46,200-46,500. Not diagnosed. It may be the bot repeatedly walking into the same spot, or may be GC or streaming. The log does not say which.
2. **Deaths clustered near 6,8,-7 and 9,8,-7.** Four deaths, all rescued (recovery works): 54,8,29 (around minute 5), 94,9,-21 (around minute 9), 6,8,-7 (around minute 12, after HP fell to 9 at minute 12), and 9,8,-7 (around minute 14). The last two are within 3 m of each other and near the fps dip in finding 1. Fights may explain the HP loss; the log does not say what killed the bot.
3. **Bot-side stuck counts rose (trips stuck 3 → 78).** These count the bot's own goal stalls (`s.soak.stuckTrips`), not NPC unsticks. NPC unsticks were **0** (`NPC unsticks: 0 total`), so no NPC stuck events were recorded.
4. **Environment, not game:** 2124 lines of `file watcher error: ENOSPC: System limit for number of file watchers reached` from the Vite dev server. The host's inotify watch limit is too low for the repo. This is not a game error. It may have added load during the run (it is not measured here). Raising `fs.inotify.max_user_watches` is a host change that needs the user's sudo.
