## Findings

1. **Frame rate dips below 30 fps (sampled).** Minute 10 read 24 fps; minutes 5, 7, 12 and 15 read 28–31 fps. The bot's fps figure is a per-minute sample on a headless browser, not a steady average, and these are not a budget failure. Log lines 7, 9, 13, 15 and 19. Worth a look on the owner's hardware with `scripts/perf-budget.mjs`.
2. **Deaths were recovered every time.** Three deaths (minutes 5, 9 to 10, and 15) were each rescued by the Aesculapian rescue. Log lines 6, 12 and 18. HP was back to 100 after each rescue.
3. **Stuck counter is ambiguous.** The per-minute `stuck` count rose from 1 to 10 (the soak's trip-stuck tally). The soak's NPC unstick report says 0 total and the `/tmp/stuck-2026-11-16.json` log is empty (`total: 0`). Either the tally counts player trips that needed a nudge, or the NPC stuck hook did not fire. The run does not say which, so this is unresolved and should be checked against the soak script.
4. **No errors.** `grep` for error, warn, Uncaught and page errors found no lines. The verdict line reports `errors 0`.

- Story or quest progression: the bot wanders, fights and saves. It does not follow the main quest or talk through dialogue trees.
- Input and controls: the soak does not exercise keyboard, trackpad or gamepad presets (use `npm run check:controls`).
